Project Overview

Molana Legal required a website that positioned the firm as trustworthy and experienced while providing clear information about legal services and making it easy for potential clients to request consultations.

The Challenge

Many law firm websites appear outdated and overly complex. The challenge was to create a clean and modern design that would communicate authority while guiding visitors toward contacting the firm.

The Strategy

The design approach emphasized clarity, structure, and credibility. Trust-building elements such as testimonials, professional imagery, and clear service explanations were integrated into the layout.

Key Features

  • Structured service sections explaining legal expertise
  • Trust-focused testimonials and credibility indicators
  • Clear consultation booking calls-to-action
  • Professional typography and balanced layout

The Result

The final design positions the firm as professional and reliable while making it simple for visitors to understand services and request a consultation.
